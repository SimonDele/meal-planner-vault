## Ingredients
- Bunch Coriander
- 2 Jalapeno
- 2 Spring Onions
- 1 clove Garlic
- 1 tablespoon White Wine Vinegar
- Juice of 1 Lime
- Pinch Cumin
- 4 tablespoons Mayonnaise

## Steps
1. Put all the ingredients in a blender or food processor and mix into a sauce. Add seasoning, then pour into a serving bowl.
